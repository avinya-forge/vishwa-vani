# Vishwa-Vani Vision

Vishwa-Vani is a comprehensive digital library and research platform for Vedic and Sanskrit scriptures. It aims to provide scholars, devotees, and researchers with a high-fidelity, interactive experience for exploring, studying, and understanding the profound wisdom of Indian spiritual heritage.

## Objectives
- **Accessibility:** Make rare and profound texts accessible to a global audience.
- **Accuracy:** Ensure the highest level of textual and translation accuracy.
- **Interactivity:** Provide tools for linguistic analysis, philosophical correlation, and cross-referencing.
- **Modernity:** Leverage modern web technologies and AI to enhance the study experience.
