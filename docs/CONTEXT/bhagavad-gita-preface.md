# Bhagavad Gita: Preface & Historical Context

**Timeline:** 3102 BCE (Start of Kali Yuga epoch).
**Formation Context:** The dialogue occurred on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, moments before the devastating Mahabharata war. It was prompted by Arjuna's moral and psychological collapse when facing his own relatives, teachers, and elders in battle.

## Historical & Textual Evidences
*   **Mahabharata (Bhishma Parva):** The primary source of the Gita, embedded sequentially from chapters 25 to 42.
*   **Astronomical References:** Planetary positions described in the Mahabharata text align with archaeological and archaeoastronomical dating models pointing to roughly 3000 BCE.
*   **Samskaras & Stotras:** Validated across subsequent Puranas and Stotras which heavily cite Krishna's discourse as the ultimate philosophical authority.

## Core Purpose
To resolve the conflict between *Dharma* (duty/righteousness) and personal attachment. It transitions Arjuna from a state of paralyzing grief to absolute clarity of action.
