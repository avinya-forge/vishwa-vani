# Bhagavad Gita: Postface & Impact

**Immediate Impact:** Arjuna regains his resolve, picks up his bow (Gandiva), and the Pandavas ultimately win the Kurukshetra war, establishing the rule of Dharma.
**Philosophical Impact:** It became the definitive synthesis of Hindu thought—unifying Karma (Action), Bhakti (Devotion), and Jnana (Knowledge). 

## Subsequent Developments
*   **Brahma Sutras & Upanishads:** Together with the Gita, it formed the *Prasthanatrayi* (the three pillars of Vedic philosophy).
*   **Commentarial Tradition:** Triggered centuries of elite philosophical debate, with profound commentaries by Adi Shankara (Advaita), Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita), and Madhva (Dvaita), each analyzing the text to support their cosmological worldview.
