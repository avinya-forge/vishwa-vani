# 📚 Vishwa-Vani: Knowledge & Learning Paths

In accordance with Hindu Philosophy, our automated pipeline extracts authentic, un-hallucinated data directly from the verified 3-Gold texts (and NEVER from open-internet scraping). The `Cognitive Brain` categorizes this data into specific structured Learning Paths.

## 1. 🗂️ The Canonical Onboarding Sequence
Future text acquisitions MUST strictly follow this philosophical hierarchy:
1. **Prasthanatrayi (The Three Sources):** Upanishads (Shruti), Brahma Sutras (Nyaya), Bhagavad Gita (Smriti).
2. **Vedas (Shruti):** Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda.
3. **Itihasas (History):** Mahabharata, Ramayana.
4. **Puranas (Mythology/Cosmology):** Bhagavata, Vishnu, Garuda, Skanda, etc.
5. **Dharma Shastras & Darshanas:** Manusmriti, Yoga Sutras, Samskaras.

## 2. 🪔 Daily Pooja & Ritual Path
**Source Texts:** *Samskaras, Stotras, Rigveda (Agni Sukta).*
*   **Morning Sandhya:** Automated extraction of Gayatri Mantra and Achamana from *Samskaras*.
*   **Stotra Recitation:** Dynamic queue pulling from *Stotras* (Gita Mahatmya, Isha Shanti Patha).
*   **Homa/Yajna Baseline:** Extraction of *Rigveda 1.1* (Agni Sukta) for fundamental offerings.

## 3. 🌕 Satyanarayan Pooja & Vratas
**Source Texts:** *Skanda Purana (Pending Onboarding).*

## 4. 📅 Hindu Calendar & Festival Importance
**Source Texts:** *Vishnu Purana, Bhagavata Purana, Garuda Purana.*
*   **Extraction Engine:** The `CognitiveBrain` scans the Puranic lake for keywords (`Ekadashi`, `Janmashtami`, `Navaratri`).
